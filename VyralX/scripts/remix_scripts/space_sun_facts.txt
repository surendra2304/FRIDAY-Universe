The Sun is losing four million tonnes of itself every single second. That vanished mass becomes sunlight, through Einstein's famous equation, E equals m c squared. A single photon born in the Sun's core can take one hundred thousand years to fight its way to the surface. Then that same light crosses all of space to reach your eyes in just eight minutes. The Sun is so huge that one point three million Earths could fit inside it. It holds ninety-nine point eight six percent of all the mass in the entire solar system. In five billion years it will swell into a red giant wider than Earth's own orbit. Every sunset you have ever seen is that giant, quietly burning. Now watch that first fact again. Four million tonnes. Every second.
