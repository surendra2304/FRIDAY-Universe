## Provenance and the honesty rule

This playbook distils the strategy cluster of the Instagram reference
corpus (faceless-channel playbooks Dd7wM1giLJ1 / Dd4D7qrgb9V /
DeMoCfwCHE8; creator-tool list DeKGOHHE0Rh; the Silk Road story series
DZP1ifcpIof by @mysterajofficial). Those videos are sales-oriented
creator content: their growth claims ("this niche prints money", "post
daily and the algorithm rewards you") are **unverified creator claims**.
They are recorded here as *leads and structure*, never as facts, and
never as performance predictions — VyralX's own metrics store is empty
(TASK-VYR-005/VYR-010), so the channel has no performance data to
predict from. When real metrics exist, this document gets revised
against them, not against more reels.

## What the corpus actually contributed

1. **Niche selection is a sourcing decision first.** The playbooks
   agree on picking niches with deep, repeatable content supply.
   Fitted to VyralX, the binding version is stricter: *lawful* supply
   at scale. The five selection criteria in the JSON (lawful supply,
   evergreen/searchable, producible in-pipeline, no gate-excluded
   categories, a series format exists) are the filter every future
   lane proposal runs through.
2. **Series beat one-offs.** Recurring structure — same format, new
   chapter — is the corpus's most consistent faceless-channel pattern,
   and it is exactly what TASK-IG-VYR-04's series automation plans.
3. **The three owner lanes already fit the filter** (confirmed by the
   owner 2026-11-15): space & science (PD footage), history /
   did-you-know (PD archives), tech & AI explainers (in-house
   diagrams). The per-lane pillars and positioning are in the JSON.

## The Silk Road seed

DZP1ifcpIof is a Telugu story series (ch. 4) on the Silk Road dark-web
market and the small digital mistakes that brought down Ross Ulbricht.
As a lane-2 topic it is ideal seed material: a true, documented,
multi-chapter narrative. The JSON carries it as a series seed —
"The Silk Road: the marketplace that changed the internet", seven
chapter topics, 2-day cadence — shaped for
`series_automation.plan_episodes`. It is a money *story* (history),
not finance content; scripts still pass the compliance gate's advice
screen like everything else.

## Creator tools (DeKGOHHE0Rh)

The corpus item curates creator-tool websites. Recorded in the JSON
as `reference_only_unverified`: the list was not verified tool by
tool, and the standing rule applies — a tool being listed, popular or
free is not a licence. Any tool adopted later gets its own terms
check, and any media from it enters through provenance
(TASK-VYR-004/IG-VYR-07), not around it.

## What this document is not

- Not a growth guarantee, in any lane, at any cadence.
- Not permission to publish: everything still flows through the

  unchanged).
- Not a substitute for the channel operator's metrics discipline

  named from data — until then, from nothing.
