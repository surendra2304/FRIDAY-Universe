## Gig description

**Your page gets one scroll to answer: "why you, why now?" Most pages never answer it.**

I write landing and sales pages structured around your buyer's problem, your proof and one clear action — headlines that stop the scroll and CTAs people click.

**What you get:**
- Complete page copy, section by section, ready for your designer or page builder
- Multiple headline options, so you can test angles
- Benefit-led bullets written from YOUR facts and YOUR real customer proof
- Objection-handling woven in (Premium adds a full FAQ section)

**How I work:** I research your offer, audience and competitors, draft with AI tools for speed, then rewrite by hand — headlines and the opening get the most human work, because they carry the page. Prefer no AI? Say so and I'll honour it.

**My process:**
1. You share your offer, audience, page link and proof.
2. I map the page structure and angle before writing a word.
3. I write and refine the full page.
4. You review — revisions included until it reads like your best salesperson.

**New-seller note:** I'm new to Fiverr and building reviews, so sales-page copy is at a fraction of agency rates. I don't write pages for financial schemes or miracle health claims.

## FAQ

**Q1. Can you guarantee my conversion rate will go up?**
No — conversion depends on your offer, traffic and price as much as copy, and any seller promising a number is guessing. I guarantee proven page structure, sharp headlines and copy built entirely around your buyer.

**Q2. Do you design or build the page too?**
No — I deliver the complete copy in a sectioned document (Premium includes wireframe notes) that any designer or page builder can implement directly.

**Q3. I have no testimonials or results yet. Can the page still work?**
Yes. We build proof differently — your process, your credentials, your guarantee, your story. What I won't do is invent testimonials or results; fake proof destroys trust and can get pages shut down.
