## Description (paste into the itch.io project description)

**Your next video doesn't need more ideas. It needs a starting structure.**

Most small channels don't stall because the creator has nothing to say — they stall
at the blank page, the weak first 30 seconds, and the upload-day details that quietly
cost views. The YouTube Creator Starter Pack removes all three.

### What's inside

1. **5 complete script templates** — listicle, explainer, story/ documentary,
   comparison and myth-busting formats. Each one is a fill-in structure with the
   hook, retention beats and payoff already mapped, plus sample lines throughout
   and a worked opening example (Template A) so you can see a template in action
   before you write a word.
2. **The 100-Hook Bank** — 100 opening lines organised by type (curiosity gap,
   bold claim, mistake/warning, number-led, question, story cold-open and more),
   90 of them with a fill-in-the-blank version for your niche.
3. **Thumbnail Text Guide** — the 3-word rule, contrast and readability checks,
   25 proven thumbnail text formulas, and the mistakes that make thumbnails
   invisible on a phone screen.
4. **The Upload Checklist** — a 27-point pre-publish checklist covering title,
   description, tags, end screens, pinned comments and the first-hour routine, so
   nothing that costs you clicks is left to memory.
5. **How-to-use guide** — a 7-day plan for turning the pack into your first (or
   next) four published videos.

### Who this is for

- Faceless channel creators (facts, tech, history, money education, lists)
- On-camera creators in their first 100 videos who script — or know they should
- Anyone whose videos lose most viewers in the first 30 seconds

### What this is not

No growth guarantees (nobody honest can promise views), no copied scripts from
existing channels, and no "algorithm hacks". This is the working structure
professional scriptwriters use, packaged so a beginner can use it tonight.

**Format:** 5 Markdown/PDF-ready documents, ~9,000 words total. Yours forever,
updates included. Made by CometWorks — the content studio behind SEO articles,
YouTube scripts and social media packs for small businesses.
