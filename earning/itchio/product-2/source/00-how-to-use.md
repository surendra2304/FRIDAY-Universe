# How to Use This Pack — Your First 7 Days

You bought structure, not homework. Here is the fastest way to turn this pack into
published videos.

**Day 1 — Pick your format.** Skim the five script templates (Document 01) and pick
the one that matches the video you already want to make. Don't pick a new idea —
pick a structure for the idea you have.

**Day 2 — Steal a hook.** Open the Hook Bank (Document 02), find your template's
hook type, and fill in two candidate hooks for your topic. Read both aloud. The one
you can say without stumbling is your opener.

**Day 3 — Fill the template.** Write your script into the template's beats. Keep
sentences short enough to say in one breath. If a section fights you, your topic is
probably two videos — split it and you've just planned next week's upload too.

**Day 4 — Thumbnail before you record.** Use the Thumbnail Text Guide (Document 03)
to write your thumbnail text (3 words or fewer) and your title. If the thumbnail
text and title say the same thing, rewrite one of them — they should complete each
other, not repeat each other.

**Day 5 — Record.** Read the script aloud once before recording for real. Mark any
sentence you trip on and shorten it. Faceless channels: record in a quiet room,
phone 20 cm from your mouth, and speak 10% slower than feels natural.

**Day 6 — Edit and upload with the checklist.** Work through the Upload Checklist
(Document 04) top to bottom as you prepare the upload. Every item on it exists
because skipping it costs views.

**Day 7 — The first-hour routine.** Publish, then do the checklist's first-hour
steps (pinned comment, community post, reply to early comments). Then plan video 2
by repeating Days 1–6 — most creators finish the second pass in half the time.

**The one rule that matters:** a published imperfect video beats a perfect script
in a drawer. This pack exists to get you to "published" faster — use it that way.
