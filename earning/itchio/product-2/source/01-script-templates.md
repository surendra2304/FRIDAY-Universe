# Document 01 — Five Script Templates That Hold Attention

Every template below follows the same skeleton — **Hook (0–30s) → Promise → Beats
with open loops → Payoff → Next-video bridge** — because retention is a structure
problem before it is a talent problem. Fill the brackets, keep sentences speakable
(one breath each), and aim for 130–150 spoken words per minute of video.

---

## Template A — The Listicle ("7 Things…" / Top-N)

**Best for:** facts, tools, mistakes, tips channels.
**Length guide:** 120–150 words per item + hook/payoff.

- **Hook:** "Number [2] is the one almost everyone gets wrong — and it's costing
  you [result]." (Open the loop: they must stay for #2.)
- **Promise:** "By the end you'll know all [N] — and the last one is the one I'd
  start with today."
- **Beats (repeat per item):** Item name (bold claim, not a label) → why it matters
  in one sentence → one concrete example or number → one-line bridge that teases
  the next item ("…but that only works if you also fix number [next].")
- **Payoff:** restate the #1 recommendation and the first action to take today.
- **Bridge:** "I made a full video on [related topic] — it's the perfect next step."

*Worked example (opening, tech niche):* "Number 2 is the setting almost every new
laptop owner leaves on — and it quietly cuts your battery life in half. Today we're
counting down seven settings to change in the first hour you own your machine…"

## Template B — The Explainer ("How X Actually Works")

**Best for:** tech, science, money-education channels.
**Length guide:** 1,000–1,400 words for 8–10 minutes.

- **Hook:** ask the question the viewer has never been able to answer simply:
  "Your phone listens to you — but not the way you think. Here's what's actually
  happening."
- **Promise:** "In the next [N] minutes, this will finally make sense — no jargon."
- **Beat 1 — The simple model:** explain the whole thing in 3 sentences using an
  everyday analogy.
- **Beat 2 — The three moving parts:** one short section each; end each with a
  "which is why…" that opens the next.
- **Beat 3 — The misconception:** "Here's the part most videos get wrong…" (this
  section earns shares and comments).
- **Payoff:** the analogy again, now fully earned + one practical takeaway.
- **Bridge:** tease the neighbouring mystery.

## Template C — The Story / Documentary ("The Rise and Fall of…")

**Best for:** history, business stories, true events (factual, non-graphic).
**Length guide:** 1,200–1,600 words for 10–12 minutes.

- **Hook (cold open at the climax):** start at the most dramatic true moment, in
  present tense, then cut: "…and to understand how he got there, we have to go
  back twenty years."
- **Act 1 — The setup:** who, where, what they wanted. End on the decision that
  changed everything.
- **Act 2 — The climb and the crack:** alternate wins and warning signs; place a
  "point of no return" beat at ~60% of runtime.
- **Act 3 — The fall / resolution:** pay off the cold open, then the consequence
  nobody quotes.
- **Payoff:** the one-sentence lesson, stated plainly.
- **Bridge:** "But this wasn't the only empire that fell this way…"

## Template D — The Comparison ("X vs Y — Which Is Actually Better?")

**Best for:** product, software, method comparisons.
**Length guide:** 900–1,200 words.

- **Hook:** "I used both for 30 days. The winner surprised me — and it's not the
  one with more features." (Adapt honestly to your real test.)
- **Promise + criteria:** name the 4 criteria you'll judge on, so viewers can
  follow the scoring.
- **Beats:** one criterion per section, both options scored, with a running tally
  mentioned aloud ("that's 2–1").
- **The twist beat:** the criterion nobody talks about that changes the result.
- **Payoff:** winner by use-case ("if you're a beginner… if you're switching…"),
  never a lazy single answer.
- **Bridge:** the deeper dive on the winner.

## Template E — The Myth-Buster ("Stop Believing These 5 Lies About…")

**Best for:** any niche with strong folk wisdom.
**Length guide:** 150 words per myth.

- **Hook:** "Everything you've been told about [topic] is backwards — starting
  with the advice in every beginner guide."
- **Beats (per myth):** state the myth as believers say it → "here's why it
  spread" (one sentence — respect earns trust) → the evidence/reality → what to
  do instead.
- **Escalation rule:** order myths from "commonly wrong" to "this one is actively
  costing you money/health/time".
- **Payoff:** the single replacement rule that covers most of the myths.
- **Bridge:** the myth too big for this video gets its own episode — tease it.

---

## Universal rules (all templates)

1. The first sentence must contain a number, a mistake, a question or a bold
   claim. If it contains none, rewrite it.
2. Say the video's promise out loud before the 30-second mark.
3. Every 60–90 seconds, open a loop or pay one off. Never do neither.
4. Write the payoff before you write the middle. A script that knows its ending
   never rambles.
5. Read it aloud once. Every sentence you stumble on gets shortened.
