# Document 02 — The 100-Hook Bank

A hook has one job: make the next 30 seconds feel un-skippable. Each hook below is
a fill-in template — replace the [brackets] with your topic. Read your finished
hook aloud; if you can say it in one breath with energy, it works.

## Curiosity gap (1–12)

1. "There's a reason [successful group] never [common action] — and it's not what you think."
2. "I found the one [thing] that [experts] don't want beginners to know about."
3. "This [object/method] looks useless. It's actually the reason [result]."
4. "What happens if you [action] every day for 30 days? The answer surprised even me."
5. "Nobody talks about the [hidden step] between [start] and [result]."
6. "The real reason [outcome] happens has almost nothing to do with [assumed cause]."
7. "I studied [N] [examples] to find out why some [succeed] and most don't."
8. "There's a [small thing] hiding in your [place/tool] that's quietly [cost]."
9. "This was banned / removed / discontinued — and the reason why changes everything."
10. "If you zoom in on [ordinary thing], you'll see something nobody notices."
11. "The first [person/group] to ever [achievement] did something you're not doing."
12. "Everything about [topic] makes sense once you know this one fact."

## How to use the bank

- Match hook type to template: Listicle → number-led; Explainer → question-led or
  curiosity gap; Story → story cold-open; Comparison → versus; Myth-buster →
  mistake/warning or bold claim.
- Write two hooks per video, record both, keep the one with more energy.
- A hook is a promise. If the video doesn't pay it off, the hook was a lie — and
  viewers remember liars. Always pay it off.
