# Document 03 — Thumbnail Text Guide

More than half of YouTube is watched on phones. Your thumbnail is not a poster —
it's a 3-word headline competing at postage-stamp size.

## The rules

1. **3 words or fewer.** Four is a compromise, five is a paragraph. Every word
   must survive being read at 10% zoom in under one second.
2. **Thumbnail text ≠ title.** They are two halves of one sentence. Title: "I
   Tried Every Budget Laptop" → thumbnail: "ONE CLEAR WINNER". If they say the
   same thing, you've wasted half your packaging.
3. **One idea, one emotion.** Curiosity ("HE QUIT?!"), tension ("DAY 30…"),
   stakes ("$10 vs $1,000"). Never two ideas.
4. **Contrast beats decoration.** Dark text on light or light on dark, a heavy
   stroke/shadow, no thin fonts, no text over busy image areas.
5. **The squint test.** Shrink your thumbnail to phone size and squint. If you
   can't read it and name the emotion instantly, it fails — no matter how it
   looks full-screen.
6. **Faces help; fake shock hurts.** A genuine expression outperforms the
   open-mouth stock pose, which viewers now read as clickbait. Faceless channels:
   one bold object, huge, beats a collage every time.
7. **Numbers and symbols are free attention.** "$", "?", "!", "0", "24H" read
   faster than words. Use at most one symbol cluster.

## 25 thumbnail text formulas

1. "I WAS WRONG" — myth-buster / reversal videos
2. "STOP DOING THIS" — mistake videos
3. "DAY 1 → DAY 30" — challenge / transformation
4. "$[X] vs $[Y]" — comparison
5. "NOBODY TALKS ABOUT THIS" — hidden-step explainers
6. "IT'S ACTUALLY SIMPLE" — demystifier videos
7. "3 MISTAKES" / "7 RULES" — listicles (number + noun only)
8. "HE QUIT." / "IT DIED." — story videos
9. "WATCH BEFORE BUYING" — reviews
10. "THE REAL REASON" — explainers
11. "I TRIED EVERY [X]" — exhaustive tests
12. "DON'T [ACTION] YET" — warning videos
13. "ZERO TO [RESULT]" — journey videos
14. "THIS CHANGED EVERYTHING" — turning-point stories
15. "CHEAP vs PRO" — versus videos
16. "STILL WORTH IT?" — current-year reviews
17. "THE HIDDEN [X]" — curiosity-gap videos
18. "I WISH I KNEW" — beginner lessons
19. "ONE SETTING" / "ONE RULE" — single-fix videos
20. "IT GETS WORSE" — escalating stories
21. "FREE > PAID" — tool comparisons
22. "NOBODY EXPECTED THIS" — result reveals
23. "LAST CHANCE?" — news/urgency (only when true)
24. "BEGINNER → PRO" — setup/progress videos
25. "WHY [X] FAILED" — post-mortem stories

## Mistakes that make thumbnails invisible

- Repeating the title word-for-word.
- Text smaller than ~1/8 of the thumbnail height.
- More than 3 visual elements (face + object + text is the maximum).
- Red-and-yellow "everything" colour schemes that blend into the feed.
- Faces looking away from the text (eyes should pull toward the words).
- Designing at full-screen size and never checking the phone-size version.
- Promising in the thumbnail what the video doesn't deliver — the click you win
  costs you the subscriber and the algorithm's trust.

## 60-second workflow

1. Write the thumbnail text first (3 words, from the formulas).
2. Write the title to complete it, not repeat it.
3. Build the image: one object/face, text huge, contrast checked.
4. Squint test at phone size. Fix or ship.
