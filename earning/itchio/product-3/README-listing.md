## Description (paste into the itch.io project description)

**Your followers are not your audience — the algorithm is.**

This starter kit is the 7-day plan for coaches and creators who want an email list they actually own. One person, one problem, one promise — then one download, one page, three emails, and five free ways to find your first 100 subscribers.

### What's inside

1. **The complete eBook (3,610 words)** — Introduction plus all six day-chapters in full:
   - **Day 1 — One Person, One Problem:** the filter that stops you building a freebie nobody wants, with three worked promise sentences.
   - **Day 2 — Turn the Promise into a Download:** pick the smallest format that delivers the result, write it in one sitting, name it after the result.
   - **Day 3 — The Landing Page That Asks for One Thing:** headline = the promise, three bullets = the outcome, one field = the email.
   - **Days 4–5 — Your First Welcome Emails:** three ready-to-adapt emails (delivery + quick win, the story behind the freebie, the soft invitation) — every one ends with a reply prompt.
   - **Day 6 — Where the First 100 Subscribers Come From:** five free sources, worked a little every day.
   - **Day 7 — From Free Download to First Sale:** the honest path to a first offer — priced small, framed as help, offered once.
2. **The Day 1 Worksheet** — the three questions, the promise sentence and the format picker on one printable page, with room to write your answers.
3. **A 20-minute action at the end of every chapter** — each day is one sitting, so the kit is finished in a week.

### Who this is for

- Coaches, creators, freelancers and consultants starting an email list from zero
- Anyone with followers but no list — and no budget for ads
- Creators who have already built a freebie nobody downloaded

### What this is not

No paid-ad playbooks (everything here is free to run), no growth guarantees, no 200-page marketing textbook. This is a short, practical plan you finish in a week — with one chapter drafted in full as the standard before you buy, and the rest written to the same standard.
