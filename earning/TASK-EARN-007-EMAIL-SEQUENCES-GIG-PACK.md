## Gig description

**Your welcome emails are the highest-opening emails you'll ever send. Most businesses waste them.**

I write email sequences that turn subscribers into readers, and readers into buyers — clear subject lines, one idea per email, one call to action, in your voice.

**What you get:**
- A complete sequence (3 / 5 / 7 emails by tier), each with subject-line options
- A deliberate arc: welcome, value, story, offer — paced across the days you choose
- Short, scannable emails written to be read on a phone
- Copy in a clean document, ready to paste into any email platform

**How I work:** I draft with AI tools for speed, then rewrite every email by hand — subject lines and calls to action get the most human attention, because they decide opens and clicks. Prefer no AI? Say so and I'll honour it.

**My process:**
1. You share your offer, audience and past emails you like
2. I map the sequence arc before writing a word
3. I write and refine the full flow
4. You review — revisions included until it sounds like you

**New-seller note:** I'm new to Fiverr, so sequence copy agencies price at $300+ is at introductory rates while I build reviews. Message me about weekly newsletters — repeat clients get a locked rate.

## FAQ

**Q1. Do you need access to my Mailchimp/Klaviyo account?**
No. I deliver the full sequence as a document with subject lines and send-order notes; you or your team paste it in. I never ask for account logins.

**Q2. Can you guarantee sales or open rates?**
No — no honest copywriter can; results depend on your list, offer and sending setup. What I guarantee is well-structured, on-voice copy built on proven sequence frameworks.

**Q3. What if I only need one newsletter, not a sequence?**
Message me — a single newsletter issue is available as a custom offer, and Standard/Premium already include newsletter issues so you can test my style.

## Buyer requirements (order-start questions)

1. What are you selling or promoting? Link to the offer/product page.
2. Who is on your list — how did they subscribe, and what do they want from you?
3. Which email platform do you use? (Copy is platform-neutral — this is just for formatting notes.)
4. Your brand voice in 3 words, plus 1–2 past emails or brands whose style you like.
5. Sequence goal: sales, bookings, content views, or community replies?
6. Any claims, topics or words I must avoid? (I don't write financial, medical or legal advice emails.)
