## 0. How this lane works

1. Find a real local business on Google Maps / Justdial with a weak or no website (dermatology clinics in Hyderabad first — both corpus reels name this exact niche).
2. Take only its public business info (name, address, phone, hours).
3. Build a finished website mockup with AI BEFORE pitching — the business sees its own site, not a sales deck.
4. Offer the site to the business: one-time build fee, optional monthly care plan.
5. First-money path: the first website sale.

## 2. Message scripts

All scripts are templates — the owner sends them in his own words, one clinic at a time. Replace [Clinic] and [Owner name] before sending. Never send two clinics the same copy-paste without checking the details.

### 2a. First outreach (WhatsApp / SMS)

> Hi, this is [Owner name] from Hyderabad. I was looking for skin clinics in [Area] and found [Clinic] — you have good timings and a real clinic, but no website of your own, so new patients only find you through directory listings.
>
> I built a finished preview website for [Clinic] — your name, address, phone and timings, with services and a call button. It is ready to look at. If you like it, I can put it live for you this week for a one-time fee (starting ₹4,999 [VERIFY]) — no monthly charge unless you want the care plan.
>
> Shall I send you the preview link?

### 2b. Follow-up 1 (Day 2 — no reply)

> Hi [Clinic] — just checking you saw my message about the preview website I made for your clinic. No cost to look at it. Happy to send the link whenever suits you.

### 2c. Follow-up 2 (Day 5 — no reply)

> Hi — last note from me on this. The preview for [Clinic] is ready whenever you want to see it. If a website is not a priority right now, no problem at all — I will not follow up again.

### 2d. After they say yes to the preview

> Great — here is the preview: [preview link]. It has your address, phone, timings and services. Have a look and tell me anything to change — the doctor's name, the services list, photos. Once you approve it, I put it live and hand it over. The one-time fee is [₹X — confirm tier before quoting].

### 2e. Call script (if calling instead of messaging)

1. "Hi, am I speaking with [Clinic]? My name is [Owner name], I work with local clinics in Hyderabad on their websites."
2. "I noticed your clinic does not have a website of its own — patients find you only on directories. I made a finished preview website for [Clinic] with your address, phone and timings."
3. "Can I send it on WhatsApp for you to look at? It takes two minutes, and there is no cost to look."
4. If yes: send the preview + follow-up 1 the next day. If no: thank them, do not push.

## 3. Follow-up sequence

| Day | Action |
|-----|--------|
| 0 | First outreach (2a) — one clinic at a time, details checked |
| 2 | Follow-up 1 (2b) if no reply |
| 5 | Follow-up 2 (2c) if still no reply — then STOP for that clinic |
| Any reply | Answer in their words; if they want the preview, send it (2d) the same day |
| Preview approved | Confirm tier + price, put live, hand over, invoice |

**Rules:** never message the same clinic more than 3 times; never pitch a clinic that asked to stop; never quote a price the owner has not confirmed; never claim the site is already live — it is a preview until the clinic approves and pays.

## 4. Objection handling

| They say | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| "We already have a website" | Apologise, check the claim — if they do have one, remove them from the list and note it. (All 10 in this batch were checked: no website of their own.) |
| "How much?" | Quote only the tier the owner confirmed. Do not invent a discount. |
| "Send it on email" | Ask for the email address; send the preview link + the tier table from §1. |
| "We will think about it" | "Of course — the preview stays ready. I will check back in a week unless you tell me otherwise." Then follow up once, on day 7. |
| "Not interested" | Thank them and stop. Mark them "declined" on the lead sheet. |
