## Gig description (unchanged from EARN-007)

**Your welcome emails are the highest-opening emails you'll ever send. Most businesses waste them.**

I write email sequences that turn subscribers into readers, and readers into buyers — clear subject lines, one idea per email, one call to action, in your voice.

**What you get:**
- A complete sequence (3 / 5 / 7 emails by tier), each with subject-line options
- A deliberate arc: welcome, value, story, offer — paced across the days you choose
- Short, scannable emails written to be read on a phone
- Copy in a clean document, ready to paste into any email platform

**How I work:** I draft with AI tools for speed, then rewrite every email by hand — subject lines and calls to action get the most human attention, because they decide opens and clicks. Prefer no AI? Say so and I'll honour it.

**My process:**
1. You share your offer, audience and past emails you like
2. I map the sequence arc before writing a word
3. I write and refine the full flow
4. You review — revisions included until it sounds like you

**New-seller note:** I'm new to Fiverr, so sequence copy agencies price at $300+ is at introductory rates while I build reviews. Message me about weekly newsletters — repeat clients get a locked rate.

### Sample 1 — 3-email welcome sequence (Basic tier shape)

**Client (fictional):** Maya, online yoga coach. Offer: free 20-minute morning-flow class.
Goal: turn new subscribers into class bookings.

**Email 1 — Welcome + the free class (send immediately)**

- Subject options: "Your free morning flow is inside" / "Welcome — let's stretch this out"
- Body:

> Hi — Maya here. Thanks for joining. Your free 20-minute morning flow is linked below —
> no mat experience needed, just 20 minutes before your day starts.
>
> [Watch the free class]
>
> Do it tomorrow morning, then reply and tell me how it felt. I read every reply.
>
> — Maya

**Email 2 — Value: the 3-breath reset (send day 2)**

- Subject options: "Stressed at 3pm? Try this (60 seconds)" / "The reset I use between classes"
- Body:

> Quick one. Between classes I do this: breathe in for 4, hold for 4, out for 6. Three rounds.
> Sixty seconds, and the afternoon feels different.
>
> Try it before your next meeting — and if you want the full version, the free morning
> flow walks you through it slowly: [Watch the free class]
>
> — Maya

**Email 3 — Story + soft offer (send day 4)**

- Subject options: "I almost quit yoga in 2019" / "Why I teach mornings now"
- Body:

> In 2019 I almost quit teaching. My own practice had gone stale, and I was going
> through the motions. What brought me back was teaching one small morning class —
> five people, no music, just breathing and moving slowly.
>
> That's the class I teach now, every weekday at 7am. If the free flow helped, the live
> morning class is where it deepens: [Book a live class — first week free]
>
> — Maya

### Sample 2 — 3-email mini-launch sequence (Premium tier shape, shortened)

> — Alex
