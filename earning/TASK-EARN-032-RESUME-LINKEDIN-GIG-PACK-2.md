## 2. Gig description (publish-ready)

A recruiter spends seconds on your resume — and your LinkedIn profile is usually the next click. I write both as one story: a resume that survives the screening software and a LinkedIn profile that makes a hiring manager want to start a conversation.

What you get:

- A resume built around what you achieved, not what you were responsible for. Bullets carry numbers where they exist — "handled complaints" is forgettable; "resolved 40+ cases a week with a 96% satisfaction score" is a reason to call you.
- A LinkedIn profile that sounds like you, not a keyword list: a headline that states your role and your value, an About section that tells your story in your own voice, and experience entries that back up the resume.
- Copy written for the screening software as well as the people: keywords drawn from real postings in your field, plain formatting that parsers read cleanly, and nothing that breaks when the file is uploaded.

How it works: send your current resume (or your work history) and the roles you are aiming at. I rewrite, you review, and I revise until the copy reads like you on your best day — the version of you a recruiter would hire.

No templates, no invented achievements, no buzzword soup — just clear, honest copy that shows what you can actually do.

## 5. FAQs (publish-ready)

1. **What do you need from me to start?** Your current resume if you have one (any format), or a list of your jobs, dates, and what you did in each. Plus the kind of roles you are targeting — job postings help most.
2. **Will my resume pass applicant tracking systems?** Yes — the rewrite uses keywords from real postings in your field and clean formatting that parsers read reliably. No tables, text boxes, or graphics that break ATS.
3. **Do you write the LinkedIn profile too?** On Premium, yes: headline, About section, and up to 3 experience entries, written to paste straight into your profile. Basic and Standard cover the resume (Standard adds a cover letter).
4. **Can you match my voice?** Yes. Share a sample of your writing, your current resume, or your LinkedIn — I will match its tone, whether formal, friendly, or direct.
5. **What if I have no resume at all?** No problem — send your work history (jobs, dates, achievements) and I will build the resume from it. Delivery adds 1 day. [VERIFY]
6. **Do you offer revisions?** Yes — 1 revision on Basic, 2 on Standard, 3 on Premium. Revisions cover wording, tone, and structure within the agreed scope.
