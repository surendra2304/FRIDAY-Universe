## 2. Gig description (publish-ready)

Your email list is the only audience you own — but only if your emails get
opened, read, and answered. I write email sequences that do that: welcome
series that turn new subscribers into buyers, nurture sequences that keep your
list warm, and sales sequences that close without sounding like a hard sell.

What you get:

- A complete sequence written in your brand voice, not a template with your
  name pasted in. Every email has one job, one clear call to action, and a
  subject line written to earn the open.
- Copy that respects your reader: short paragraphs, plain language, and a
  reason to keep reading in every email.
- Delivery as a formatted document, ready to paste into Mailchimp, Klaviyo,
  ConvertKit, ActiveCampaign, or any email platform you use.

How it works: you tell me about your business, your audience, and what the
sequence should achieve. I draft the full sequence, you review it, and I revise
until it sounds like you on your best day.

No hype, no fake urgency, no spam-trigger words — just emails your subscribers
will actually want to open.

## 8. Sample work (demonstrates the service — fictional examples)

### Outline A — 3-email welcome sequence (Basic tier shape)

1. **Welcome + story.** Subject: "Welcome — here's what happens next".
   Introduce the brand, set expectations, one small first step.
2. **Value + proof.** Subject: "The one thing most people get wrong about X".
   Teach something useful, show a real result, no selling.
3. **Soft offer.** Subject: "Ready when you are". Recap the value, present
   the offer once, clear single call to action.

### Outline B — 5-email nurture sequence (Standard tier shape)

1. **Problem.** Name the reader's problem in their own words.
2. **Agitate.** Show what the problem costs if left alone.
3. **Solution.** Introduce the approach (not the product yet).
4. **Proof.** A result, a story, a testimonial — one, told well.
5. **Offer.** The product as the natural next step, with a deadline only if
   a real one exists.
