## Why this gig

Podcasters record every week and run out of things to say in the same order — hook, story, point, close. A script removes the rambling and the retakes, and show notes are what search engines actually index, so one order serves both the recording and discovery. A podcaster who orders one episode script returns for the next episode, so this is repeatable work with the same creator audience as the email packs. Delivery is a document — no podcast-platform or hosting login ever needed.

**Who it's for:** Solo podcasters, interview shows and small business shows that record regularly and want episodes that sound prepared instead of improvised. Scripts are written for the ear — short sentences, spoken transitions, no walls of text.

## Gig description

**Your listeners decide in the first thirty seconds whether to stay. Most episodes waste them.**

I write podcast scripts that sound like you on your best day — a clear hook, one idea at a time, and a close that tells listeners exactly what to do next. No rambling, no filler, nothing you would never actually say out loud.

**What you get:**
- A complete episode script (length by tier), written to be read aloud
- Show notes with timestamps and an SEO-friendly title (Standard and Premium)
- Title options for the episode, so you can pick the strongest hook
- Promo copy to post when the episode goes live (Premium)

**How it works:**
1. You send the topic, the main points, your audience, and a link to a past episode if you have one
2. I draft the script in your voice — send a sample of your speaking style and I will match it
3. You record, and revise with me until it sounds right

## Requirements from the buyer

- Episode topic and the 2–3 main points to cover
- Target episode length (minutes) and audience
- Tone (conversational, teaching, interview, storytelling)
- A past episode link or a voice sample, if available
- Any links, names or calls to action to include in the show notes

## FAQ

**Do you write interview scripts too?**
Yes — I write host intros, guest questions in a natural order, and transitions, plus the outro. Send the guest's background and I will build the question flow around it.

**Will the script sound like me?**
That is the point. Send a past episode or a short voice sample and I match your phrasing and pace. The first draft is where we tune that — revisions are for exactly this.

**What do show notes include?**
An SEO-friendly title, a short episode summary, timestamps for the main segments, and any links or calls to action you provide.

**Can you write a whole season?**
Yes — order the Premium tier for two episodes to start, then message me about a recurring arrangement for the season. Repeat buyers get the same voice, episode to episode.
