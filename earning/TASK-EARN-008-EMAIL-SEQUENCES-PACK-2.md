## Gig description

**Your shoppers already picked the product. Most stores never email them back.**

I write recovery email sequences that bring cart abandoners and lapsed buyers back to checkout — clear subject lines, one idea per email, one call to action, in your voice.

**What you get:**
- A complete recovery sequence (3 / 5 / 7 emails by tier), each with subject-line options
- A deliberate arc: reminder, value, objection-handling, incentive (only if you offer one), last call — paced across the days you choose
- Short, scannable emails written to be read on a phone
- Copy in a clean document, ready to paste into any email platform

**How I work:** I draft with AI tools for speed, then rewrite every email by hand — subject lines and calls to action get the most human attention, because they decide opens and clicks. Prefer no AI? Say so and I'll honour it.

**My process:**
1. You share your store, your checkout flow and any discount you offer
2. I map the recovery arc before writing a word
3. I write and refine the full flow
4. You review — revisions included until it sounds like you

**New-seller note:** I'm new to Fiverr, so recovery-sequence copy agencies price at $300+ is at introductory rates while I build reviews. Message me about win-back campaigns — repeat clients get a locked rate.

## FAQ

**Q1. Do you need access to my store or my Klaviyo/Mailchimp account?**
No. I deliver the full sequence as a document with subject lines and send-order notes; you or your team paste it in. I never ask for account logins.

**Q2. Can you guarantee how many carts I'll recover?**
No — no honest copywriter can; recovery depends on your traffic, prices, checkout friction and the offer. What I guarantee is well-structured, on-voice copy built on proven recovery frameworks.

**Q3. Do I have to offer a discount to recover carts?**
No. Many carts recover on reminder + reassurance alone. If you do offer one, tell me the code and terms and I'll weave it in; I never invent a discount on your behalf.

**Q4. I don't have an email list yet — is this gig for me?**
Probably not yet — start with the welcome-sequence gig (Gig 5) to build the list, then come back for recovery once you have traffic. Message me and I'll point you to the right gig.
