## Episode description (paste into the podcast host)

Most small businesses don't fail because the product is bad — they fail because nobody ever finds them. In this episode: three things you can do this week, with no ad budget, to get found, get trusted, and turn one customer into the next ten. Be findable where people already look, ask for referrals the right way, and show up where your customers already gather — plus a listener question on whether you need to be on every social platform at once. (You don't.)

## Three takeaways

1. **Be findable:** claim your free Google Business Profile, answer every review, and keep your business name, address and phone number identical everywhere.
2. **Turn one customer into the next:** ask for a referral at the moment of delight, name one specific person or situation, and give your customer something easy to forward.
3. **Show up where your people already are:** one real partnership with a complementary local business beats ten networking events — and one useful answer a week beats any slogan.

## Homework (from the outro)

1. Claim or tidy up your Google Business Profile.
2. Ask one happy customer for one specific introduction.
3. Message one neighbouring business about a joint offer.
