# Sample Product Descriptions — E-Commerce Before/After

**Purpose:** Portfolio sample for the CometWorks Contra profile (profile-pack.md §5, item 4).
**Blurb on the profile:** "Three store listings rewritten from feature lists into benefit-led copy with natural keyword placement — the before/after shows exactly what my descriptions service does."
**Format:** Three listings — a Shopify-style physical product, a WooCommerce-style handmade product, and a Payhip-style digital product. Each AFTER is 100 words or fewer including its title (the Starter tier: $20, up to 100 words each), with the target keyword placed where a shopper would naturally search for it. The products are illustrative samples written for this portfolio, not listings for real brands; no reviews, sales figures or guarantees are claimed.
**How to read it:** BEFORE is the kind of bare feature list a busy seller pastes in. AFTER is the same product, rewritten to lead with the benefit, answer the buyer's silent objections, and carry the keyword — without adding a single claim the product can't back up.

---

## Listing 1 — Insulated water bottle (Shopify-style)

**Target keyword:** insulated water bottle

### BEFORE (the seller's feature list)

Stainless steel water bottle. 750 ml. Double-wall vacuum insulation. Keeps drinks cold 24 hours, hot 12 hours. Leak-proof lid. Fits car cup holders. BPA-free. Available in black, blue, silver.

### AFTER (97 words)

**Insulated Water Bottle — Cold for 24 Hours, Fits Your Car Cup Holder**

One bottle, all day, no lukewarm regrets. This insulated water bottle keeps water ice-cold for 24 hours and coffee hot for 12, so your morning drink still tastes right at 5 pm. No condensation rings on your desk, and the leak-proof lid means it rides in your bag next to your laptop without a second thought. At 750 ml it holds enough for a workout or a long commute, and it slides into a car cup holder. BPA-free stainless steel, in black, blue or silver.

### What changed, and why

- **Feature → benefit:** "double-wall vacuum insulation" became "no condensation rings on your desk"; "leak-proof lid" became "rides in your bag next to your laptop without a second thought".
- **Objection handled:** the two quiet worries about a bottle — will it leak, will it sweat — are answered inside the copy instead of left to the reviews.
- **Keyword:** "insulated water bottle" appears in the title and the first sentence, where a searcher and a skimmer both land first.

## Listing 2 — Handmade lavender soy candle (WooCommerce-style)

**Target keyword:** handmade soy candle

### BEFORE (the seller's feature list)

Soy wax candle. 220 g. Cotton wick. Lavender scent. Burn time approx 45 hours. Glass jar with lid. Handmade.

### AFTER (99 words)

**Handmade Lavender Soy Candle — 45 Hours of Calm in a Glass Jar**

Light it when the day finally stops asking things of you. This handmade soy candle burns clean and even for around 45 hours, filling a room with real lavender — present, not overpowering, and gone when you blow it out instead of hanging around like a headache. Soy wax and a cotton wick mean no black soot on your walls and no wax wasted at the edges. Glass jar, lidded — dust-free between burns, shelf-worthy when it's not. Poured in small batches; when this batch is gone, it's gone.

### What changed, and why

- **Feature → benefit:** "45 hours" became "45 hours of calm"; "glass jar with lid" became "dust-free between burns, shelf-worthy when it's not".
- **Objection handled:** "will the scent be too strong?" and "will it soot my walls?" — the two reasons people hesitate over a candle they can't smell first — are answered before checkout.
- **Keyword:** "handmade soy candle" opens the title and returns in the first line, so the listing reads as the thing a buyer searched for, not a page stuffed with it.

## Listing 3 — Notion budget planner template (Payhip-style digital product)

**Target keyword:** budget planner template

### BEFORE (the seller's feature list)

Notion budget template. Monthly budget tracker, expense categories, savings goals, debt tracker. Instant download. Works with free Notion account.

### AFTER (98 words)

**Notion Budget Planner Template — See Where Your Money Actually Goes**

Budgets fail because they take an evening to set up and nobody opens them again. This budget planner template takes ten minutes: add your income once, sort spending into categories, and the monthly view shows where the money went — no formulas to write, no spreadsheet skills needed. Savings goals and a debt tracker share the page, so the full picture is one glance. It works with a free Notion account, downloads instantly, and you keep it forever. If you can type a number, you can run this budget.

### What changed, and why

- **Feature → benefit:** "expense categories" became "the monthly view shows where the money went"; "works with free Notion" became "no formulas to write, no spreadsheet skills needed".
- **Objection handled:** the real reason budget tools get abandoned — setup effort and ongoing friction — is named in the first line and answered by the "ten minutes" promise and the "one glance" layout.
- **Keyword:** "budget planner template" leads the title and returns in the opening line; "Notion" stays attached to it, matching how buyers actually search.

## A note on the numbers

Word counts above were measured on the AFTER text including its title (97 / 99 / 98 words). Nothing in the rewrites invents a claim: burn time is stated as "around 45 hours", insulation times are the seller's own figures carried over from BEFORE, and no review, ranking or sales promise appears anywhere. If a seller's real specs differ, the copy changes with them — the structure is the deliverable, not the numbers.
