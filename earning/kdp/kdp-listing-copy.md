## Book description (paste into the KDP description field)

**Your followers don't belong to you. Your email list does.**

An algorithm change, a hacked account, a platform deciding your niche is "low
quality" — any of them can erase years of audience-building overnight. An email
list is the one audience you actually own. And you don't need 10,000 people on it.
You need your first 100 *right* people — the ones who reply, buy and stay.

This book builds that list with you, one finished piece per day:

**Day 1** — Pick the one person and the one problem (the decision every failed
list gets wrong)
**Day 2** — Build a lead magnet people genuinely want, in one afternoon
**Day 3** — The signup page that converts, on free tools
**Day 4** — A 5-email welcome sequence, written email by email
**Day 5** — The free-tier tools setup, including the settings that keep you out
of spam
**Day 6** — Your first 50 subscribers without ads or spam tactics
**Day 7** — From 50 to 100, and the weekly rhythm that keeps the list alive

Plus a final chapter on making your first honest offer to a small list — no fake
scarcity, no bought testimonials, no hype.

**Who this book is for:** coaches, creators, freelancers and small-business owners
starting from zero subscribers, with no ads budget and no desire to spam anyone.

**Who it is not for:** anyone looking for bought lists, automation tricks or
guaranteed income. This is the slow, honest way — which is why it works.

Every chapter ends with a worksheet or template, and the appendices collect them
all: the avatar worksheet, lead-magnet planner, signup-page copy template, the five
welcome-email skeletons and the launch-post scripts. Seven days from now, you
won't have "learned about" email lists. You'll have one.
