# KDP eBook — Full Manuscript Outline

**Working title:** Your First 100 Subscribers
**Subtitle:** A 7-Day Plan to Build an Email List That Actually Replies — For Coaches,
Creators and Small Businesses Starting From Zero
**Author name on KDP:** Surendra (pen name "CometWorks" is the publisher/imprint line)
**Source material:** expanded from the CometWorks lead-magnet pack "The 7-Day Email
List Starter Kit" (EARN-004/EARN-010 material, repo `earning/products/lead-magnet-ebook/`)
— the free short version becomes this book's funnel; the book is the complete,
paid-depth edition. No content is copied from any third-party work.
**Target length:** ~25,000 words · **Format:** Kindle eBook first (paperback optional later)
**Promise of the book:** in seven days of small, finishable sessions, a beginner with
no list and no audience budget builds a working lead magnet, a signup page, and a
5-email welcome sequence — and sends the first invitation to real people.

## Chapter-by-chapter outline

**Introduction — Why 100 subscribers beat 10,000 followers** (~1,500 words)
The rented-land problem (algorithms, account risk), what an email list actually is
in business terms, why the first 100 are the hardest and the most valuable, how to
use this book (one chapter = one day = one finished asset), and the honesty box:
what this book cannot do (no bought lists, no spam, no guaranteed income).

**Chapter 1 — Day 1: Pick the One Person and the One Problem** (~2,500 words)
[FULL DRAFT FILED: chapter-01-draft.md] The reader avatar exercise, the problem
interview (5 conversations), narrowing from niche to one painful problem, the
one-sentence list promise, and the Day-1 worksheet.

**Chapter 2 — Day 2: Build a Lead Magnet People Actually Want** (~3,000 words)
The four lead-magnet formats that work for beginners (checklist, template, mini
guide, email course), the "one problem, one sitting" rule, building it in an
afternoon with free tools, title formulas, the quality bar (would a stranger trade
an email for it?), and the Day-2 production checklist.

**Chapter 3 — Day 3: The Signup Page That Converts** (~3,000 words)
Anatomy of a one-page signup form (headline, 3 bullets, one field, one button),
free page options compared honestly, the two-sentence privacy promise, mobile-first
checks, what conversion rate to expect and what to fix first when it's low.

**Chapter 4 — Day 4: Your Welcome Sequence, Email by Email** (~4,000 words)
The 5-email arc (deliver + story, teach, teach + proof of concept, invite, ask a
question), subject-line patterns, the 80/20 value-to-offer ratio, full structural
templates for each email, the reply-bait final email that starts conversations.

**Chapter 5 — Day 5: The Tools Setup, Free Tier Only** (~2,500 words)
Email platforms compared on free tiers and deliverability, connecting the form to
the list, the welcome-sequence automation setup step by step, the three settings
that decide whether you land in spam (SPF/DKIM explained in plain words), test-send
protocol.

**Chapter 6 — Day 6: Your First 50 Subscribers Without Ads** (~3,500 words)
The warm-circle launch (and how to ask without being spammy), content swaps and
guest spots, the signature/link-everywhere sweep, community participation rules
that don't get you banned, the one-post-per-platform launch kit with scripts.

**Chapter 7 — Day 7: From 50 to 100 — and What to Send Every Week** (~3,000 words)
Doubling what's already working, the weekly newsletter rhythm (one email, three
content sources), subject-line testing for tiny lists, cleaning the list without
fear, the metrics that matter at 100 subscribers (replies > open rates).

**Chapter 8 — The First Offer: Selling to a Small List Honestly** (~2,500 words)
Why 100 right people out-buy 10,000 strangers, the soft-offer email structure,
pricing a first small product/service, the ethics chapter (no fake scarcity, no
rented testimonials), what to do when nobody buys (diagnose, don't despair).

**Appendix A — Worksheets and templates** (~1,500 words)
Day-1 avatar worksheet, lead-magnet planner, signup-page copy template, the five
welcome-email skeletons, launch-post scripts.

**Appendix B — The 30-day calendar** (~500 words)
Days 8–30 at a glance: two emails a week, one growth action a week, one review.
