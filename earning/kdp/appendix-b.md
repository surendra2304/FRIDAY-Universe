# Your First 100 Subscribers — Appendix B (Draft)

## Appendix B — The 30-Day Calendar

The 7-day plan got you to 100 subscribers — or close. Days 8–30 keep the
list warm without taking over your week. The rhythm is simple: **two emails
a week, one growth action a week, one review.**

| Week | Emails (2 per week) | Growth action (1 per week) | Notes |
|------|---------------------|----------------------------|-------|
| Week 2 — Days 8–14 | Email 1: teach one thing from a reader reply. Email 2: share a small, honest result — yours or a reader's, with permission. | Repeat your best-performing launch action from Day 6: re-ask your warm circle, or revisit the one community where people actually replied. | Aim to answer every reply within a day. Replies are the list warming up. |
| Week 3 — Days 15–21 | Email 1: answer the most common question your subscribers have asked. Email 2: teach the next step after the lead magnet. | One content swap or guest spot: offer something useful to someone else's audience, in exchange for a mention of your signup page. | If a subject line flops, note it and move on. Tiny lists give noisy signals — one week is not a trend. |
| Week 4 — Days 22–28 | Email 1: a short story about a problem you solved this week. Email 2: your first soft offer (Chapter 8) — or, if you sent it in Week 3, a single plain reminder that it is still there. | Go back to where your subscribers actually came from: spend the week in that one place, helping, and mention the signup page once. | Do not send the offer more than twice in total. A third nudge costs more trust than it earns in sales. |
| Days 29–30 | — | **The review (one, at Day 30).** | See below. No new emails and no new growth action on these two days. |

### The Day-30 review

Set aside an hour on Day 30 and answer these questions in writing. Do not
judge the month by subscriber count alone — count conversations, not just
names.

1. **How many subscribers do I have, and how many joined in Days 8–30?**
   Where did the new ones come from — which action actually worked?
2. **How many replies did I get, and what did people ask about?**
   List the three most common questions. Those are your next three emails.
3. **Which emails were opened and replied to, and which were ignored?**
   Keep doing what got replies. Stop doing what got silence — or fix one
   thing about it, not everything at once.
4. **Did anyone unsubscribe, and when?** A few unsubscribes after an offer
   email are normal. A steady trickle after every email means the promise
   and the content have drifted apart — re-read your Day-1 promise.
5. **What is the one thing I will change next month?** Pick one: a better
   lead magnet, a clearer signup page, a different email day, or a new
   growth action. One change, tested for a month, beats five changes tested
   for a week.

### After Day 30

Keep the same rhythm next month: two emails a week, one growth action a week, one review at month's end.
