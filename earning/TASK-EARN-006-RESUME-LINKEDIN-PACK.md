## 2. Gig description (publish-ready)

Your resume has about six seconds to earn a second look — and your LinkedIn
profile is the first thing a recruiter checks after it. I write both so they
work together: a resume that gets past the screening software and a LinkedIn
profile that makes a hiring manager want to meet you.

What you get:

- A resume rewritten around results, not duties. Every bullet shows what you
  achieved, with numbers where they exist — because "managed a team" is
  forgettable and "led a team of 6 to cut delivery time 30%" is not.
- A LinkedIn profile that reads like a person, not a keyword list: a headline
  that says what you do and who you help, an About section that tells your
  story in your voice, and experience entries that match the resume.
- Copy written for the applicant tracking systems as well as the humans:
  keywords from real postings in your field, clean formatting, no gimmicks
  that break the parser.

How it works: you send me your current resume (or your work history) and the
kind of roles you are targeting. I rewrite, you review, and I revise until it
sounds like you on your best day — the version of you a recruiter would hire.

No templates, no inflated claims, no buzzword soup — just clear, honest copy
that shows what you can actually do.

## 4. How the order works (buyer-facing process)

1. You place the order and answer the requirement questions (§7).
2. I confirm your target roles and current materials — and ask any follow-up
   questions within 12 hours.
3. I rewrite and deliver by the tier's delivery time.
4. You review and request revisions (per your tier). The order completes when
   the copy sounds like you and targets the roles you want.

## 5. FAQs (publish-ready)

1. **What do you need from me to start?** Your current resume if you have
   one (any format), or a list of your jobs, dates, and what you did in each.
   Plus the kind of roles you are targeting — job postings help most.
2. **Will my resume pass applicant tracking systems?** Yes — the rewrite
   uses keywords from real postings in your field and clean formatting that
   parsers read reliably. No tables, text boxes, or graphics that break ATS.
3. **Do you write the LinkedIn profile too?** On Premium, yes: headline,
   About section, and up to 3 experience entries, written to paste straight
   into your profile. Basic and Standard cover the resume (Standard adds a
   cover letter).
4. **Can you match my voice?** Yes. Share a sample of your writing, your
   current resume, or your LinkedIn — I will match its tone, whether formal,
   friendly, or direct.
5. **What if I have no resume at all?** No problem — send your work history
   (jobs, dates, achievements) and I will build the resume from it. Delivery
   adds 1 day. [VERIFY]
6. **Do you offer revisions?** Yes — 1 revision on Basic, 2 on Standard,
   3 on Premium. Revisions cover wording, tone, and structure within the
   agreed scope.
