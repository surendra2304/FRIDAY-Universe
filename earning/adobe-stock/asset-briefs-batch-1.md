# Adobe Stock — Asset Briefs, Batch 1 (20 assets)

Generation rules for every brief: photorealistic or clean-illustration style as
stated, no people or faces, no text/words in the image (stock buyers add their own),
no logos or branded products, no watermarks. Save as high-resolution JPG.
The "AI label" declaration is checked at upload for all 20 — see the batch plan.
