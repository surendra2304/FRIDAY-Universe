## Why this lane, and the rules that keep it safe

- Adobe Stock is one of the few major stock libraries that **accepts generative-AI
  content — provided it is honestly labelled**. Every upload in this lane must have
  the **"Created using generative AI tools"** declaration checked and, where the
  upload flow asks, the AI category selected. Unlabelled AI uploads risk account
  termination — the label is non-negotiable.
- **Never** upload AI content to Shutterstock, Getty/iStock, Alamy or Freepik —
  those platforms prohibit AI uploads (ban + earnings forfeiture). This lane is
  Adobe Stock only.
- AI assets must not depict real, identifiable people, real brands/logos,
  trademarked characters, real editorial events, or misleading "photojournalism".
  All 20 briefs in batch 1 are objects, scenes, textures and concepts — no faces,
  no logos, no real locations presented as news.
- Free-only: generation uses free-tier tools only; the contributor account is free;
  Adobe pays royalties out of sales (standard royalty 33% for images). Payout via
  PayPal or Payoneer once the balance reaches the $25 payout threshold — the owner
  connects the payout method inside the account when the first sales land.

## The starter batch strategy

Stock income is a volume + search game: buyers find assets by keywords, so batch 1
targets **evergreen commercial concepts** with year-round demand and low seasonality
risk:

- Remote-work and small-business scenes (laptop desks, invoices, calendars) — the
  highest-volume business category.
- Seasonal-neutral celebration/abstract backgrounds (bokeh, confetti, gradients) —
  used by designers all year.
- Food and wellness flat-lays (no people) — blog and social demand.
- Texture/pattern packs (paper, marble, fabric) — perennial designer demand.

Each brief in `asset-briefs-batch-1.md` is written to be generated in any free image
tool: subject, composition, lighting, negative prompt (what to exclude), and the
commercial use the buyer has in mind. `titles-keywords-batch-1.md` is the upload
metadata: title + up to 49 keywords per asset, in spreadsheet-ready table form —
copy each row into the upload CSV or paste fields directly.

## Cadence after batch 1

- Batch size 20, one batch per week while the lane is young (review times for AI
  content can run days to weeks; staggered batches keep the pipeline full).
- Track acceptance rate per category; double down on whichever category clears
  70% acceptance and produces the first downloads.
- First-money path: upload → review/acceptance → buyer licences an asset →
  royalty lands in the contributor balance → payout at $25 via the owner's chosen
  method. Realistic first income: weeks to a few months — this is the slow,
  compounding lane, deliberately paired with the faster Fiverr/Contra lanes.

## Honest timeline (read before counting on this lane)

Adobe Stock pays 45 days after the first sale. This is a seed lane: December money
is NOT expected from it. Uploads must first pass Adobe's review (days to weeks for
AI content), then a buyer must licence an asset, then the 45-day payout clock runs,
and payout itself needs the $25 balance threshold. Treat any income from batch 1 as
a 2027 compounding trickle, not near-term revenue.
