## Why this gig

Every business with an email list needs a lead magnet, and most of them have a freebie nobody finishes — too long, too vague, or a sales pitch in disguise. A short eBook that delivers one clear result is the magnet that actually gets read, and a reader who finishes it trusts the next email. A buyer who orders one magnet returns for the next one, so this is repeatable work with the same small-business audience as the email packs. Delivery is a document — no email-platform or website login ever needed.

## Gig description

**Your lead magnet has one job: make a stranger hand over an email address. Most freebies never do it.**

I write short eBooks built around one clear promise — a hook on page one, one useful result per chapter, and a close that points the reader to your next step. No padding, no sales pitch in disguise, nothing a busy reader would abandon halfway.

**What you get:**
- A complete lead-magnet eBook (length by tier), written to be finished in one sitting
- Title and subtitle options, so you can pick the strongest hook
- Cover and back-cover copy, plus a call-to-action page (Standard and Premium)
- Promo copy to post when the magnet goes live (Premium)

**How it works:**
1. You send the topic, the one result the reader should walk away with, your audience, and where the reader goes next
2. I outline the eBook first, so you approve the shape before I write a word
3. You get the manuscript as a clean document, and revise with me until it sounds like you

## Requirements from the buyer

- eBook topic and the one result the reader should get from it
- Audience (who it is for) and the offer or next step it leads to
- Tone (teaching, conversational, practical, inspirational)
- Brand voice in 3 words, plus a sample of existing content if available
- Any facts, figures or links to include — no invented statistics

## FAQ

**Do you design the eBook too?**
No — delivery is the manuscript as a clean document. Cover and back-cover copy are included (Standard and Premium); layout and design are for your designer or a tool like Canva or Beacon.

**Will it match my voice?**
That is the point. Send a sample of your content and I match your phrasing and pace. The outline comes first, so we agree the shape before the writing starts — revisions are for tuning the voice.

**How long should a lead magnet be?**
Short enough to finish in one sitting. That is why the tiers top out at ~5,000 words — a magnet that gets read beats a 50-page guide that gets filed. If you need a full-length book, message me for a custom offer.

**Can you guarantee signups?**
No — signups depend on your traffic and your offer, not the eBook alone. What I guarantee is a clear, useful eBook built around one promise, written to be finished.

**Can you write in my niche?**
Yes, if you send source material — your outline, past posts, or the facts the eBook must cover. I write from your material; I never invent expertise, testimonials or statistics.
